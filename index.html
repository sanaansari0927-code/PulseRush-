How To Play

1
Start the Trial

Select your difficulty mode and press START GAME. A 3-2-1 countdown will synchronize your focus before the 60-second trial starts.

2
Detect the Reactor Target

A random panel in the 3x3 matrix will illuminate with high-voltage cyan energy and trigger an acoustic tone.

3
Strike with High Speed

Click, tap, or press the matching keycap as rapidly as possible. Reactions faster than 250ms receive immense speed bonuses.

4
Avoid Penalties & Stale Hits

Clicking dark inactive panels incurs a point penalty and snaps your streak. Failing to hit before deadline counts as a timeout miss.

5
Build Monster Combos

Chain 5, 10, 15, and 20 consecutive hits to charge the combo bar up to 3.0x Multiplier and earn streak milestone bonuses.
